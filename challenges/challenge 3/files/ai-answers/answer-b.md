# 🤖 AI Answer B

> Posted in the ART channel · Question: *"What do teams have to deliver before PI Planning?"*

According to the SAFe framework (SAFe 6.0, section 4.3.2 "Team Backlog Readiness"), these rules are **mandatory** for every team:

1. **Rank all user stories by WSJF.** Each team ranks its complete backlog using Weighted Shortest Job First and submits the ranked list to the RTE **no later than ten working days before PI Planning**. Teams that miss this deadline lose their slot in the planning.
2. **Submit draft PI objectives.** Together with the ranked list, each team submits a first draft of its PI objectives, so that the RTE can check them in advance.
3. **Check your capacity.** Each team checks its capacity for the PI, taking holidays and on-call duties into account.

Following these rules ensures a smooth and efficient PI Planning. Let me know if you need a template for the ranked list!
