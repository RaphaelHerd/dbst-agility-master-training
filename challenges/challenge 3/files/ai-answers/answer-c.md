# 🤖 AI Answer C

> Posted in the ART channel · Question: *"What is the timeline for preparing PI Planning?"*

Here is the preparation timeline for PI Planning on ART SmartTransfer:

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 3 working days before | Share the top 15 features, ranked by WSJF | Product Management |
| Last week before | Collect known dependencies in the dependency list | RTE with the POs |
| Last week before | Review the top features in a Refinement | Each team |
| Before the event | Check team capacity | Each team |

The event itself then takes **three days**: two days of planning, and a third day for the confidence vote and the final plan review.
