# 🤖 AI Answer A

> Posted in the ART channel · Question: *"How does PI Planning work on our train?"*

PI Planning on ART SmartTransfer is a **two-day event**, held in person.

On **day 1**, after the business context and the vision, the teams go into breakouts. Each team drafts its **PI objectives** and a first plan. At the end of the day, every team presents its draft plan in a **10-minute slot**.

On **day 2**, the teams adjust their plans, and the **business owners** assign business value from 1 to 10 to each team's PI objectives.

The event closes with a **confidence vote** using fist of five. If the average is below 3, the RTE and the teams agree on how to rework the plan before the event ends.
