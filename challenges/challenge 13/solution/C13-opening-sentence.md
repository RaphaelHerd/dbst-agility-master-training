## 🎤 Our Choice

> **"The train is ready for PI Planning with one honest change: the feed v2 rollout moves one PI, and three decisions from you today keep everything else on track."**

29 words: the status, the change and the ask, in one breath.

## 🗑️ What We Didn't Choose, and Why

| Candidate from Kiro | Why not |
|---|---|
| *"All four teams are on track for a successful PI Planning."* | Not true: Depot missed its goal, and the rollout moves |
| *"In twelve weeks, feed v2 will be live at all major stations."* | Tuesday's forecast, no longer valid with two people fewer |
| *"We face serious risks that threaten the whole PI."* | Alarming, and it gives the committee nothing to decide |
| *"Today we present our status, our forecast, our priorities, our risks and our decisions."* | An agenda, not a message |
