# Our AI Working Agreement

Team Signal on ART SmartTransfer · version 1.0 · agreed by the team, the PO and the Agility Master · shared with the works council before it takes effect

## Why
Kiro helps us draft faster. People stay responsible for every result: AI drafts, people decide.

## What We Use Kiro For
- Drafts: updates, notes, mails, agendas and checklists
- Summaries and checks of our own documents
- Never for decisions about people, or to analyze an individual's performance or behavior

## 1. Data Minimization
- Only the data the task needs. We clean exports before Kiro sees them, for example by deleting columns in Excel.
- No names: pseudonyms (Member A, B, C) only where the task needs a person. No health or private details, and no judgments about people.
- [DB placeholder: data classes allowed in prompts]

## 2. Transparency
- Everyone who receives a result from us can see that AI helped: our team, other teams, stakeholders and Rail Ops.
- Anyone in the team can ask how a result was made.

## 3. AI Labels
- Every result that leaves the team carries the label "AI-assisted draft · reviewed by: role, team".
- No labels on chat messages or on drafts that stay in the team.
- [DB placeholder: company labeling rule]

## 4. Review Gate
- Drafts that stay in the team: the author checks them.
- Everything that leaves the team or feeds a decision: a named reviewer checks every statement and every number against the sources before release.

## 5. Audit Log
- One log line per released result: prompt, sources, version, reviewer.
- We log results, not people: no recording of chats, and the log is never used to evaluate anyone.

## 6. Bias and Fairness Check
Before a result affects people or decisions, we ask for other perspectives, use neutral wording, check who is missing in the data and let a second person read it.
