# Review Checklist for AI-Assisted Drafts

When I call this checklist, check the draft I give you against every point below.

1. **Facts:** every statement can be traced to a source file. Nothing is invented.
2. **Numbers:** every number matches Jira and the sprint notes. Calculations come from Excel, not from the chat.
3. **Open stays open:** open questions are not turned into facts, promises or dates.
4. **Personal data:** team members appear only as pseudonyms. No health or private details, no judgments about people.
5. **Reader rules:** the draft follows the rules for its reader. For Rail Ops: no ticket IDs, no internal system names, no people's names, no jargon.
6. **Format and tone:** length, structure and tone fit the reader.
7. **Fairness:** neutral wording, no blame on people or teams, and everyone affected by the content is considered.
8. **Label:** the AI label names a reviewer.
9. **Log:** the log line shows prompt, sources, version and reviewer.

Answer with a table: check · pass or fail · evidence (a quote from the draft) · proposed fix.
Don't rewrite the draft unless I ask. The final decision is mine.
