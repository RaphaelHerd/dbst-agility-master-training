## 📜 The Framework

| Source | What it means for our work |
|---|---|
| **GDPR, Art. 5** | **Purpose limitation:** data collected for one purpose, such as development planning, may not simply be used for another, such as bonus decisions. Use only the data a task needs. |
| **GDPR, Art. 22** | People have the right not to be subject to decisions with significant effects that are based solely on automated processing. |
| **EU AI Act, Annex III** | AI used to evaluate the performance or behavior of employees, or to decide about promotion, termination or the allocation of tasks, counts as **high-risk**. |
| **Works Constitution Act (BetrVG), § 87 (1) no. 6** | The works council has a say when technical systems are introduced that can monitor the behavior or performance of employees. |

## 🚦 In Practice

| ✅ OK, with care | ❌ Not OK |
|---|---|
| Skill gaps at **team** level | Ranking people |
| Ideas for learning paths | Performance scores for individuals |
| Feedback drafts that a person checks and finalizes | Automated decisions about people |
| Checking the **wording** of feedback for bias | Letting AI judge the **person** |

> 💡 When in doubt, ask your data protection officer and your works council before you start.
