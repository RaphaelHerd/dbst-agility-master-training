# 🔄 C06 - Retro Script: Circle of Influence · Sample Solution

> 🏷️ **AI-assisted draft** · reviewed by: Agility Master, Team Signal

**Why this format:** the same topics keep coming back, and several of them sit partly outside the team. The Circle of Influence separates what the team **controls** from what it can only **influence**, and from what it has to **accept**. That stops the "same problems, nothing changes" loop.

**Setup:** the 40 notes, already clustered into topics; a whiteboard with three circles: *Control*, *Influence*, *Accept*.

## 🗓️ Agenda for 60 Minutes

| Minutes | Step | What the facilitator does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | **Check-in** | Each person says one word for the sprint. No discussion |
| 5 to 15 | **Show the topics** | Present the seven topics with notes and votes. Point out the topics that came back from earlier retros |
| 15 to 30 | **Sort into circles** | The team places each topic in *Control*, *Influence* or *Accept*. Disagreement is welcome; place the topic on the border |
| 30 to 45 | **Dive deep** | Take the three topics with the most votes. Use the five powerful questions below |
| 45 to 55 | **Decide actions** | At most three actions, each with an owner, a measure and a review date. Only from *Control* or *Influence* |
| 55 to 60 | **Check-out** | "How useful was this retro, from 1 to 5?" |

## ❓ Five Powerful Questions

| Type | Question |
|---|---|
| **Scaling** | "On a scale from 1 to 10, how much did waiting for test data slow us down, and what would make it one point better?" |
| **Exception** | "When did we last get test data on time? What was different then?" |
| **Circular** | "If the provider sat in this room, what would they say we could do differently?" |
| **Miracle** | "Imagine next Wednesday has no meetings at all. What would you do with the time, and what would we have to stop?" |
| **Commitment** | "What is one thing in our control that we will try before the next retro, and how will we know it worked?" |

## 🔁 Variants

- **Daily:** once a week, open with the scaling question for one minute. Write the number down and watch the trend.
- **Sprint Planning:** sort the sprint's risks into the three circles before the team commits.
