## 👋 Welcome

Over the next two days, you will solve **14 challenges** taken straight from an Agility Master's working week. Status updates, retrospectives, forecasts, Jira queries, PI planning, dependencies across teams: all the usual work, with one difference. **You will do it together with an AI assistant.**

This course does **not** teach you how to write a status report or facilitate a retro; you already know that. It focuses on two questions:

- 🎯 **Where** can AI save you time and improve your results?
- 🧠 **How** do you stay in control, so that the AI supports your decisions instead of making them?

## 📖 The Case: Ten Days to PI Planning

**ART SmartTransfer** is ten working days away from its next **PI Planning**. SmartTransfer is a passenger app that helps rail travellers make their connections: real-time platform information, transfer times and alternatives when a train is late.

**Kim**, the Release Train Engineer, has asked every Agility Master to get their team ready, and to try **Kiro** as an AI assistant along the way, *as long as it is used safely.*

**Your role:** every group plays the **Agility Master of Team Signal**, the team behind the real-time train data. The other three teams appear whenever the train needs them.

## 🛡️ Ground Rules for Working with AI

These rules apply to **every** challenge:

| | Rule | What it means in practice |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🔒 | **Fictional data only** | Work only with the course files. <span style="color:#EC0016;font-weight:700">Never paste real company data, names or customer data into Kiro during this course.</span> |
| ✂️ | **Share only what the task needs** | Replace people with pseudonyms (Member A, B, C). Leave out health information and judgments about people. |
| 🔍 | **Check before you trust** | Verify facts against the sources. Let Excel do the math; never take numbers from the chat at face value. |
| 🏷️ | **Label AI-assisted work** | Every result carries a label, e.g. *AI-assisted draft · reviewed by: Agility Master, Team Signal*. |
| 🚦 | **Pass the review gate** | Nothing leaves your team without a human review. |
| 📝 | **Keep a light log** | For each result, note the prompt, sources, version and reviewer. |
| 🙅 | **No profiling** | No person-level analysis and no ranking of people. Ever. |

> ⚠️ **At work, your company's AI policies always come first.**
