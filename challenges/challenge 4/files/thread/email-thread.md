## ✉️ 4 · Robin

**From:** Robin, Works Council · **Sent:** Wednesday, 08:30

Dear all,

The works council supports a responsible trial. Our conditions:

1. **No personal data in prompts:** no names, no health information, no judgments about colleagues.
2. **No analysis of individual performance or behavior**, neither by the AI nor from AI logs.
3. **Transparency:** colleagues and stakeholders must be able to see when AI was involved in a document that concerns them.
4. **A named person checks** every result before it leaves a team.
5. **The works agreement on AI applies.** Please involve us before your team agreements take effect, and whenever they change.

Kind regards,
Robin
