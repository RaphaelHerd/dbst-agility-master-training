## ⚖️ 2 · The Legal Framework in Brief

| Source | What it means for our work |
|---|---|
| **GDPR** | Process only the personal data a task needs (data minimization, Art. 5). Health data is a special category with extra protection (Art. 9). |
| **EU AI Act** | AI used to monitor or evaluate the performance and behavior of employees counts as **high-risk** (Annex III). Certain AI uses come with transparency duties (Art. 50). |
| **Works Constitution Act (BetrVG)** | The works council has a say when technical systems are introduced that can monitor the behavior or performance of employees (§ 87 (1) no. 6). |

> ℹ️ This is an overview, not legal advice. When in doubt, ask your data protection officer and your works council.

## ✅ 3 · Minimum Content of Every Team Agreement

Each team's AI working agreement covers at least:

1. **Data minimization:** which data may go into a prompt
2. **Transparency:** who is told when AI was used
3. **AI labels:** which results carry a label, and what it says
4. **Review gate:** who checks results before they leave the team
5. **Audit log:** what is logged for each result, and what is never logged
6. **Bias and fairness check:** how results that affect people or decisions are checked
7. **Company rules:** a reference to the company's AI policies in section 1
