# 🧰 Setup 01 - Kiro Workspace and First Contact

> 👤 **On your own** &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🗓️ **Day 1** · before C01 &nbsp;|&nbsp; 🛠️ **Kiro:** sign-in, workspace, chat

## 📖 Context

For the next two days, you are the **Agility Master of a team on ART SmartTransfer**. Every challenge happens in one Kiro workspace: the **course folder**. Before C01 starts, you need that workspace, and you should have felt, first-hand, the difference between a lazy prompt and a good one.

This is a guided setup, not a challenge. Follow the steps in order, on your own laptop. Even if your group later works on one screen, everyone needs a working Kiro.

## ✅ Your Tasks

### 🔑 Step 1 - Sign In

Open the Kiro desktop app and sign in. Kiro offers Google, GitHub, AWS Builder ID or your organization's identity; your trainer tells you which one to use. Continue when you see the welcome screen.

### 📁 Step 2 - Open the Course Folder

1. In Kiro, choose **File → Open Folder** and select the **course folder**: the folder that contains `README.md`, `introduction` and the challenge folders.
2. Check the file explorer on the left: you should see `README.md`, `introduction` and `challenge 1` to `challenge 14`.
3. Create your **results folder**: right-click the empty space in the file explorer, choose **New Folder** and name it exactly `results`. Every result of the course goes here.

You don't need to create anything else. Kiro creates the folder `.kiro/` by itself as soon as you save your first steering file in Setup 02.

Keep this one workspace open for both days. Don't open a second one later.

> 📚 **Further reading:** if your screen looks different, Kiro's official docs show the current interface: [Installation](https://kiro.dev/docs/getting-started/installation/) and [Your first project](https://kiro.dev/docs/getting-started/first-project/).

### 🗣️ Step 3 - Ask the Same Question Three Ways

Open the Kiro chat and ask the same question **three times**, worded three different ways. Keep all three answers visible:

1. 🔤 **Keywords:** `retro ideas team same problems`
2. 💬 **A full question:** `Which retrospective formats work for a team that keeps discussing the same problems?`
3. 🎭 **With a role:** `Acting as an experienced Agile coach on a SAFe release train, which retrospective formats would you suggest for a team that keeps discussing the same problems, and why?`

> 💡 **Tip:** Don't judge the formats yet. In **C06 · The Retro Nobody Wants Again**, you design the real retro. Right now, you only compare how differently Kiro answers the same question, depending on how you ask.

### 📝 Step 4 - Write Your Comparison Note

In a few bullet points, note what changed across the three answers: how specific, which tone, how useful, and what surprised you.

Save it as a file, not just as a chat message:

1. Right-click the `results` folder and choose **New File**. Name it exactly `S01-first-contact-notes.md`.
2. Paste your bullet points and save the file (**Ctrl+S** on Windows, **Cmd+S** on Mac).

This is a normal Markdown file, not a steering file. You'll want it again when the course covers the 7 principles of prompt design.

> 💡 Not sure what it should look like? [`task-01/first-contact-notes.example.md`](task-01/first-contact-notes.example.md) shows an example. Your notes will read differently, because Kiro's answers vary.

### ✔️ Step 5 - Confirm You're Ready

Tell your trainer that your workspace is open and all three answers are visible. The group moves on when everyone is ready.
