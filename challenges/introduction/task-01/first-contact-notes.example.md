# 📝 Example: First-Contact Notes

> An example of what Step 4 can look like. Your own notes will read differently, because Kiro's answers vary from run to run. Use it to see *what kind of things* to notice, not as content to copy.

**Keywords (`retro ideas team same problems`):**
- A short list of popular formats, such as Start/Stop/Continue and the Sailboat, with no explanation.
- No question back to me: Kiro simply guessed what I meant.

**A full question:**
- More coherent: each format came with one sentence on how it works.
- Still generic: nothing about *why* a team keeps discussing the same problems.

**With a role:**
- More specific and more confident in tone.
- It started with the cause: actions without owners, and problems outside the team's control. Then it suggested formats that address exactly that.
- It read like advice from a coach, not like a list from a book.

**What surprised me:** the role changed the *content*, not just the tone. The third answer found the real problem behind my question; the first two only answered the words.
