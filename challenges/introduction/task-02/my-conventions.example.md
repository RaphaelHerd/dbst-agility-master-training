# 🎨 Example: `my-conventions`

> An example to spark ideas, with a short explanation for each rule. Write your own: the file should reflect how *you* like to work. Copy only the rules themselves into your steering file, not the explanations.

| Rule for Kiro | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| **Tone:** short and direct. Lead with the answer, then the reasoning. No introductions like "Great question!" | Saves reading time, and you see at once whether the answer fits |
| **Structure:** use headings and bullet points for anything longer than three sentences. Put a one-line summary at the top | Long answers stay easy to scan |
| **Length:** keep answers short unless I ask for detail, or unless a short answer would hide an important trade-off | You decide when to go deep |
| **Numbers:** never calculate a number yourself. Give me the Excel formula and explain it | Language models don't really calculate; Excel does |
| **Unclear requests:** if something is unclear, ask me before you answer | One question saves a whole wrong answer |
| **Language:** answer in English, even if I write in German | The course material is in English |

> 💡 **Keep it short.** A steering file is read in every chat. Ten clear rules work better than a page of wishes.
