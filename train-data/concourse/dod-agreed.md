# 📱 Team Concourse - Our Definition of Done

> Team wiki · Team Concourse · Agreed in the Sprint 9 Retrospective · Applies on top of the ART-wide minimum in the PI Planning Playbook

A story is done when:

1. Code and design are reviewed by a second person.
2. The change is tested on iOS, Android and the web.
3. Accessibility is checked by our accessibility specialist.
