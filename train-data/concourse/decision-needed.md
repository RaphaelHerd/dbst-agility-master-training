# 📱 Decision Needed: Who Approves Notification Texts?

> Note from Jo, Product Owner, Team Concourse · input for the steering committee paper

## ❓ The Decision

Who has the final say on the texts of push notifications: Rail Ops or Team Concourse?

## 📋 Background

- Push notifications for platform changes are live for 200 beta users.
- The final notification texts (CON-305) are waiting for approval from Rail Ops. Rail Ops wants to approve every text before release.
- The release date of app 3.2 is not fixed yet. App store reviews can take up to a week.
- Our team rule: every text a passenger sees is written in plain language and checked by UX.

## ⚖️ Options

| Option | What happens | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Rail Ops approves every text** | Each new or changed text waits for Rail Ops | Rail Ops keeps full control. Every text change can delay a release |
| **B · Rail Ops approves templates** | Rail Ops approves a set of text templates once. We write new texts within them, checked by UX | Fast and consistent. Rail Ops controls the rules, not every single text |
| **C · The team decides** | We write and release texts, checked by UX. Rail Ops is informed | Fastest. Rail Ops may not support texts it hasn't seen |

## ✅ My Recommendation

**Option B.** Rail Ops keeps control of what matters, and we no longer wait for every single text.

Jo
