# 🛠️ Decision Needed: Ad-Hoc Requests

> Note from Charlie, Product Owner, Team Depot · input for the steering committee paper

## ❓ The Decision

Should all ad-hoc requests to Team Depot go through our new request form from the next sprint, with incidents as the only exception?

## 📋 Background

- In Sprint 14, ad-hoc requests took around 40 % of our capacity. The sprint delivered 2 of 5 items.
- Our PI focus is at risk: the logging migration and the monitoring rules for the delay feed.
- Member C is introducing a request form for ad-hoc requests. Without backing from the train, requests will keep bypassing it.
- All teams rely on our platform, pipelines, monitoring and on-call support.

## ⚖️ Options

| Option | What happens | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Keep things as they are** | Requests keep arriving directly | No change for requesters. Around 40 % of our capacity stays unplanned |
| **B · Request form with weekly triage** | All requests go through the form, and I triage them once a week. Incidents still go straight to on-call | Planned work is protected. Non-urgent requests can wait up to a week |
| **C · Request duty** | One engineer per sprint handles all ad-hoc requests | The rest of the team is protected. We lose one engineer's capacity every sprint |

## ✅ My Recommendation

**Option B,** backed by the steering committee, so that requests from all teams use the form.

Charlie
