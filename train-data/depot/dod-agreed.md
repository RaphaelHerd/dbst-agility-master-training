# 🛠️ Team Depot - Our Definition of Done

> Team wiki · Team Depot · Agreed in the Sprint 9 Retrospective · Applies on top of the ART-wide minimum in the PI Planning Playbook

A story is done when:

1. Everything is automated: built and deployed through the pipeline, with no manual steps.
2. Monitoring and alerts are in place for every new or changed service.
3. The change is documented in the runbook.
